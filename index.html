FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our blanks, sizing, printing, and returns.

What makes your 240+ GSM blanks different from ordinary shirts?
Standard mass-market graphic tees use 140–160 GSM lightweight blanks that curl at the collar and distort after two washes. Our 240+ GSM combed ringspun cotton retains a structured, boxy drape, sits cleanly over all body types, and provides a dense canvas that prevents graphic cracking over dozens of wash cycles.
How fast will my order arrive in the US, Canada, UK, or Europe?
Every order is routed to the certified printing facility closest to your shipping address (USA, Canada, UK, or European Union). Average transit time is 3 to 5 business days for US and Canadian orders, and 4 to 7 business days for the UK and Europe, complete with real-time door-to-door tracking.
How do The Tee Drop shirts fit? Should I size up?
Our shirts are cut with an intentional modern relaxed streetwear silhouette featuring slightly dropped shoulders and a flat-laying 1-inch ribbed collar. If you want a comfortable everyday relaxed fit, order your normal true-to-size. If you prefer a pronounced oversized skate look or gym pump cover fit, we recommend ordering one size up.
What is your 30-Day Reprint Guarantee policy?
We stand behind every single print run. If your item arrives with any manufacturing flaw, misprinted artwork, transit damage, or sizing issue, our support team will reprint and dispatch a brand-new replacement completely free of charge within 30 days of delivery.
