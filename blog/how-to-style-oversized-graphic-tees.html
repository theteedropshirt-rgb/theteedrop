How to Style Oversized Graphic Tees (Without Drowning in Fabric)

By Sam R., Creative Lead • October 24, 2026 • 3 min read

The oversized graphic tee is one of the most versatile staples in modern menswear and unisex fashion. But there is a fine line between looking intentionally relaxed and looking like you borrowed someone else’s shirt by accident.

Rule 1: Proportional Balance

If your top has an extra-roomy boxy drape, balance it with tapered or straight-leg bottoms. Cargo pants with subtle ankle tapering, raw selvedge denim, or tailored athletic shorts keep your silhouette balanced.

Rule 2: Let the Graphic Anchor the Outfit

Our designs are intentionally bold. Keep the rest of your outfit muted: chalk white sneakers, charcoal or washed black trousers, and minimalist accessories allow the artwork to speak for itself without visual clutter.

Rule 3: Mind the Shoulder Seam

A true drop-shoulder cut is designed to fall 2 to 3 inches below your natural shoulder point while maintaining a collar that stays flat against your neck. You get the relaxed drape without looking sloppy.
